About

Spero Technologies is a small software company run by Scott Driggers. We make apps and release them as open source.

Our first app is Roam, a Roku remote for Mac, iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Vision Pro, and Apple TV. It can play your TV's audio through your headphones, it's under 8 MB, and it opens in about half a second. The code is on GitHub.
